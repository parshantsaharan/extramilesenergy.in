Description

Product Description
The EVE 32140 15Ah 3C LiFePO4 Battery Cell is a high-performance cylindrical LFP cell designed for EV, electric mobility and high-power battery-pack applications.

With a 3.2V nominal voltage and 15Ah capacity, each cell provides approximately 48Wh of nominal energy. Its 3C discharge rating corresponds to a continuous discharge capability of up to 45A, making it suitable for applications that require higher current output and strong power delivery.

Important Note
3C on a 15Ah cell = 45A continuous discharge capability. Actual usable current and performance depend on the cell's specific datasheet, temperature, BMS, pack configuration and operating conditions. For a commercial listing, I would recommend using the exact EVE batch/datasheet for claims such as cycle life, internal resistance, dimensions and maximum charging current rather than assuming generic 32140 specifications.
