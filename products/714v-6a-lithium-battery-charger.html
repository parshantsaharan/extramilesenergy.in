6. COMPATIBILITY NOTE
⚠️ Important
Before purchasing or connecting this charger, make sure that your battery pack is specifically designed for a 71.4V maximum charging input.
Battery chemistry and series configuration determine the required charging voltage. Do not select a charger only on the basis of the battery's nominal voltage.
Always verify:
- Battery chemistry
- Number of cells in series
- Maximum charging voltage
- BMS charging-voltage setting
- Charger polarity
- Battery manufacturer's recommended charging voltage
Using an incorrect charging voltage can damage the battery or BMS.
