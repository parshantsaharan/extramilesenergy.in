Extra Miles Energy 51.2V 150Ah LiFePO₄ Battery – BYD Blade Cell
The Extra Miles Energy 51.2V 150Ah LiFePO₄ Battery is a high-performance lithium iron phosphate energy storage solution designed for solar power systems, hybrid inverters, home energy storage and commercial backup applications. Built using premium BYD Blade LiFePO₄ cells, this battery delivers excellent safety, long service life and reliable performance for everyday energy storage requirements.
With a nominal voltage of 51.2V and a capacity of 150Ah, the battery provides approximately 7.68 kWh of usable energy storage, making it an ideal choice for homes, offices, shops, commercial establishments and solar backup systems.
🔋 Premium BYD Blade LiFePO₄ Cells
This battery is built using BYD Blade LFP cell technology, known for its high safety, robust construction and long operating life. LiFePO₄ chemistry offers significantly better thermal stability compared with conventional lithium chemistries, making it highly suitable for stationary energy storage applications.
⚡ Long Life – Up to 6000+ Cycles
The battery is designed for up to 6000+ charge-discharge cycles under suitable operating conditions. Its long cycle life helps reduce the frequency of battery replacement and makes it a cost-effective solution for long-term solar and backup power applications.
🛡️ Smart BMS Protection
An integrated Smart Battery Management System (BMS) continuously monitors the battery and provides protection against:
- Overcharge
- Over-discharge
- Over-current
- Short circuit
- Over-temperature
- Cell voltage imbalance
The Smart BMS also supports battery monitoring and communication features such as Bluetooth, CAN and RS485, depending on the configuration.
📊 Smart Display
The battery is equipped with a digital display that allows users to monitor important battery information such as voltage, current, SOC and other operating parameters, making system monitoring easier.
☀️ Ideal for Solar & Hybrid Inverters
The 51.2V battery voltage makes this battery particularly suitable for 48V-class solar and hybrid inverter systems. It can be used for storing excess solar energy during the daytime and supplying stored energy when solar generation is unavailable.
