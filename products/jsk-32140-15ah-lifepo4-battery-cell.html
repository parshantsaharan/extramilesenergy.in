Product Description
The JSK 32140 15Ah LiFePO4 Battery Cell is a high-quality cylindrical LFP cell designed for electric vehicles, e-rickshaws, electric scooters and other high-performance battery applications.

With a 3.2V nominal voltage and 15Ah capacity, each cell provides approximately 48Wh of nominal energy. The cylindrical 32140 format makes the cell suitable for assembling different battery configurations according to the required voltage and capacity.

LiFePO4 chemistry provides a combination of high thermal stability, safety and long service potential, making it a popular choice for EV and energy-storage battery packs.
