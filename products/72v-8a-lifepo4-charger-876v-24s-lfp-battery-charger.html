Description

Product Description
Charge your 72V LiFePO4 battery pack efficiently with this 8A LFP battery charger, designed for compatible 24S LFP battery configurations.

The charger provides a regulated 87.6V DC output at 8A, which is suitable for charging a 24S LiFePO4 battery pack to its full rated voltage. With a wide AC 160V–280V, 50/60Hz input range, it is designed to operate reliably across varying mains voltage conditions.

Its robust metal enclosure and finned cooling design help with heat dissipation during operation, while the included power cable and charging connector provide a convenient charging solution.

Ideal for compatible 72V LFP battery applications, including electric vehicles, e-rickshaws, scooters and other lithium-powered systems.
