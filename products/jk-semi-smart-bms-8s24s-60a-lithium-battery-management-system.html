Description

The JK-BD6A24S6PD 60A Smart Active Balance BMS is designed for advanced lithium battery pack management in EV, e-rickshaw, electric scooter, energy storage and custom battery applications.

Supporting 8S to 24S battery configurations, this BMS can be used with different battery pack voltages depending on the selected cell chemistry and series configuration. For a 24S LiFePO4 battery, for example, it can be used in high-voltage 72V-class battery systems.

The major advantage of this model is its 0.6A active balancing technology. Rather than wasting balancing energy as heat like conventional passive balancing, active balancing transfers energy between cells to help improve voltage consistency and battery utilization.
