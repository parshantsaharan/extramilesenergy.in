Description

Product Description
The JBD 4S 100A LiFePO4 Hardware BMS is designed to protect and manage 4S LiFePO4 battery packs used in high-current applications.

A 4S LFP battery consists of four LiFePO4 cells connected in series, giving a nominal pack voltage of approximately 12.8V. The BMS provides protection against overcharge, over-discharge, over-current and short circuit, while temperature monitoring and cell balancing help maintain safe and consistent battery operation.
