🔧 Tubular Inverter Battery Maintenance (Step by Step)

Agar aapke paas lead-acid ya tubular battery hai, to ye steps follow karein:

💧 1. Watering (Distilled Water)

  • Kab dalen? Har 45–60 din mein (Hisar ki garmi mein har 30 din)
  • Kaise dalen? Battery ki cells kholkar — water level plates se thoda upar hona chahiye
  • Kya dalen? Sirf distilled water — normal water kabhi nahi
  • Kya nahi karna? Overfill mat karo — charging ke time acid overflow hoga
✅ Pro Tip: Hisar mein summers me evaporation fast hoti hai — isliye June–August me har 20–25 din check karo.

🧼 2. Terminal Cleaning

  • 🔧 Terminals par white/blue powder (corrosion) dikhe to turant saaf karo
  • 🧴 Baking soda + water mixture se saaf karo, phir Vaseline ya terminal grease lagao
  • ⚡ Loose connection check karo — battery life ke liye harmful

🌬️ 3. Ventilation

  • Battery ko ventilated jagah par rakho
  • Charging ke time garmi hoti hai — air circulation zaroori
  • Hisar ki garmi me battery direct sunlight mein na ho

⚡ 4. Charging Habits

  • Battery ko full discharge hone mat do — 50% se neeche jane se pehle charge karo
  • Long time idle rakhoge to sulfation ho jayegi
  • Inverter ka charging voltage setting sahi ho (14.4V–14.8V)

🌡️ Hisar ke Hisab se Special Maintenance Tips (2026)

Hisar ka weather extreme hai — garmi me 45°C+, sardi me 2–3°C. Isliye alag se tips:

☀️ Summer Tips (April–September)

  • 🌊 Watering frequency: Tubular me har 20–25 din me check karo
  • 🧊 Ventilation: Battery ko cool place me rakho — agar possible ho to exhaust fan near battery area
  • ⚡ Charging: Garmi me battery slightly fast charge hoti hai — but overcharge na ho
  • 🧼 Cleaning: Dust terminal par jama na ho — garmi me corrosion risk ↑
  • ⚡ Lithium: Extra Miles Energy lithium 6000+ cycles ke saath garmi me bhi stable

❄️ Winter Tips (November–February)

  • 🌡️ Cold effect: Lead-acid battery ki capacity sardi me 20–30% kam ho jati hai — ghabrana nahi
  • ⚡ Charging: Sardi me battery ko full charge rakhna — sulfation se bachayega
  • 💧 Water: Sardi me evaporation kam — isliye har 45–60 din me check karo
  • 🧣 Lithium: LiFePO4 sardi me bhi stable rehti hai — 6000+ cycles, 15–20 years life

🌧️ Rainy Season (July–September)

  • 💧 Water seepage: Battery area me leakage na ho — rain water dangerous
  • ⚡ Terminals: Nami se corrosion — grease zaroor lagao
  • 🐜 Insects: Kide makode battery vent me na chale jayein

❌ 5 Deadly Mistakes Jo Battery Khatam Kar Dete Hain

🚫 1. Normal water dalna — Tap water me minerals hote hain jo battery ki plates khatam kar dete hain. Sirf distilled water use karo.
🚫 2. Overcharging ya undercharging — Inverter ki setting sahi nahi to battery life kam. Tubular ke liye 14.4V–14.8V ideal.
🚫 3. Battery ko full discharge hone dena — 50% se neeche jane se pehle charge karo. Deep discharge = permanent damage.
🚫 4. Terminals clean na karna — Corrosion se current flow kam, battery heat, failure.
🚫 5. Ventilation ignore karna — Hisar ki garmi + no air = battery swelling, life half.
